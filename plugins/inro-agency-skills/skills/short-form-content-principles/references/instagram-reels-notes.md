# Instagram Reels: platform notes

Facts that change how you script and post a reel. Instagram changes these often. Each line says where it comes from; anything marked **check** should be confirmed in the Instagram app or Instagram's help center before you rely on it for a client.

Last reviewed: November 2026.

## Posting

| Point | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Hashtags | Up to 5 on a post or reel. Instagram moved to this from December 2025, rolling it out gradually. Fewer, specific tags. | Instagram, 18 Dec 2025, reported by Social Media Today: https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/ (**check** the app if a caption is refused) |
| Caption length | 2,200 characters, hashtags included | Inrō post composer |
| Labels at publish time | The AI label, the paid partnership label and trial reels can only be set before a post goes live | Inrō help center, https://help.inro.social/en/articles/15631977 |
| Trial reels | Shown to non-followers first; graduate manually in the Instagram app or automatically if it performs well | Inrō help center and publishing tools |
| Reel visibility | A reel can show in Feed and Reels, or in Reels only | Inrō post composer ("Feed and Reels" / "Reels only") |
| Cover | Pick a frame or upload a cover image; the cover is what people see on the profile grid | Inrō post composer |
| Cross-posting | Posting to Facebook Pages or TikTok from Inrō isn't available yet | Inrō help center |
| Reel length and reach | In June 2024 Instagram said it wouldn't recommend reels over 90 seconds. Reels can run to 3 minutes since January 2025, and reports differ on how long one can be and still be recommended. Shorter is the safe default for tip and offer reels. | Social Media Today, June 2024: https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-clarifies-advice-on-single-word-ctas-and-longer-reels/718151/ (**check** current limits) |

## Reach

| Point | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Testing in small groups | A new reel is shown to a small set of people first and pushed wider when they watch and react | General understanding, no Instagram source on file (**check**) |
| Strong signals | Instagram's head has named watch time, likes and sends (shares by DM) among the most important signals | Adam Mosseri, January 2025, reported by Social Media Today: https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/ |
| Originality | Instagram favours original content in recommendations; reposted or watermarked clips from other apps do worse | Instagram for Creators, recommendations and originality: https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-Originality |
| Engagement bait | Asking for comments only to farm interactions can limit recommendations. Using comments and DMs to talk to people and generate leads isn't treated as bait | Social Media Today, June 2024 (link above), for both sentences; Meta Transparency Center on engagement bait lowering distribution: https://transparency.meta.com/enforcement/taking-action/lowering-distribution-of-problematic-content/ |

## Comments and DMs

| Point | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| One private reply | One DM per comment from an automation, within 7 days of the comment | Inrō help center, https://help.inro.social/en/articles/10456326 |
| Opening the conversation | After the first DM, the person must reply or tap before more messages go through; then a 24-hour window opens | Same |
| Non-followers | Get the DM in message requests ("Requested" in Inrō) until they accept | Same |
| Links | Send links as a tappable link card; raw links in DM text may get throttled | Inrō help center, https://help.inro.social/en/articles/15632060 |
| Keyword matching in Inrō | Ignores case and accents | Inrō help center, https://help.inro.social/en/articles/10267476 |

## Screen layout

- Instagram lays the caption, account name and buttons over the bottom and right of a reel. Keep on-screen text and faces in the upper and middle part of the frame. General practice; exact safe-zone sizes vary by device, so **check** on a phone preview.
- Reels are vertical (9:16).
