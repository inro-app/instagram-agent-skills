# Writing rules for a client report

The client reads this document to decide whether the retainer is worth it. Every sentence has to survive the client checking it.

## Numbers

- **Never invent or estimate a number.** No figure without a tool response behind it, or the agency's word, labelled as theirs.
- **A number handed to you is not a source.** If the agency gives you a figure from an older report or from memory, re-derive it from the account where you can. Where you cannot, label it "figure supplied by [agency]".
- **Every number is scoped.** It says what it counts and which period. "820 times your automations ran in October" is scoped. "820 executions" is not.
- **A number with no comparison is not an insight.** Compare with last month, or with the client's own baseline, or label it a total.
- **Totals to date are labelled "to date".** Never present an all-time figure as a monthly one.
- **Summed post reach is "combined reach".** The same person is counted once per post they saw.
- **No outcome promises.** No projections, no "you will get", no platform averages presented as targets. Projections belong in a pitch or a plan, not a monthly report.

## Inrō measures up to the click

Inrō sees the DM conversation and the tap on a tracked link. It does not see what happens on the client's website afterwards.

| Never write | Write instead |
| --- | --- |
| 190 bookings | 190 people clicked through to your booking page |
| Your DMs made 4,000 in sales | 230 people clicked through to your shop from a DM |
| 12% conversion to customer | 12% of the people who got the DM tapped the link |

The exceptions are payments taken through Inrō's Stripe step and bookings made through Inrō's Calendly step: those are recorded, and you can write "34 people paid in the DM" or "18 calls were booked from the DM".

## A zero is a claim about Inrō, not about the client

Inrō records what Inrō did. It cannot see another automation tool, an email list or a CRM somewhere else. A zero in the data means "no record in Inrō", which is not the same as "this never happens".

**Scope every zero to what Inrō can observe.**

| Never write | Write instead |
| --- | --- |
| You sent no campaigns | No campaign was sent from Inrō in October |
| Nobody answers your story replies | No automation in Inrō picks up story replies yet |
| You capture no email addresses | No email address is stored on a contact in Inrō yet |
| The AI agent did nothing | The AI agent is switched off in Inrō this month |
| No one clicked | No tracked link in Inrō was clicked in October |

**A zero that is the finding still leads.** "1,900 people commented on your posts in October and no automation in Inrō replied to them" is a strong, checkable line. The force is in the volume and the gap, not in absolutes.

**Absences Inrō cannot see at all** (an email list, a CRM, a team member answering DMs on their phone) are not yours to assert. Write what the data holds.

## Explain drops

A drop with a reason is a normal sentence. A drop left unexplained reads as a problem being hidden.

- "Fewer people reached out this month: 6 posts went out instead of 14."
- "Automations were paused for 3 days while the Instagram connection was restored. The paused runs were sent once it was back."
- "Clicks fell after the offer ended on the 15th."

If you do not know why something dropped, say what you checked and ask the agency before writing it. Never guess a cause in the document.

## Their language, not the tool's

| Tool word | Client word |
| --- | --- |
| Scenario, execution | Automation, times it ran |
| Conversion link click | People who clicked through to [destination] |
| agent_reply, AI step | Replies from your AI assistant |
| Activated contacts | People your automations messaged this period |
| first_interaction | New people who reached out |
| Folder | Group, or list |

Name each automation by what it does: "the flow that DMs your price list to people who comment PRICE", not its internal title.

## The document describes findings, not the process

State what was measured and what it showed. Never narrate who looked, how, or how hard it was.

| Never write | Write instead |
| --- | --- |
| We went through your inbox | Inbound messages were grouped by topic |
| We pulled your numbers from Inrō | Figures for 1 to 31 October |
| We decided to leave stories out | Story figures were not available this month |

No meta-commentary on your own reasoning. Notes for the agency (a figure left out and why, a doubt about a number, what to check next month) go in your message to the agency, never in the document.

## Privacy

- No contact names, handles, emails or phone numbers in the document.
- Quote a message only when it is a stock phrase or a button tap ("send me the link"). Paraphrase anything a person wrote in their own words.
