# Client brand-voice file

Fill this once per client and save it where you keep that client's files (a Claude Project for the client is a good home, so it's there every session). Paste or attach it when you ask for a caption. Update it when the client gives feedback, so the next caption doesn't repeat the miss.

Leave anything you don't know blank. Blank means "ask me" for the essentials (marked *) and "use judgment" for the rest.

```markdown

## Account
- Instagram username*: @
- Inrō account id (partner connector only):
- Time zone* (a city-based zone, e.g. Europe/Madrid, America/Chicago, Australia/Sydney):
- Language(s) for captions*:
- Usual posting windows (days and local times):

## Who we're talking to
- The one person a post is for (age range, situation, what they want, what's in their way):
- What they already know, so we don't explain it:
- What they're tired of hearing from businesses like this one:

## How we sound
- Voice in three words*:
- We sound like (a person, not a brand: "a friendly head chef", "a straight-talking coach"):
- We say / words we like:
- We never say / words and phrases to avoid*:
- Emojis: none / a few / these ones only:
- Caption length: one-liners / short (2 to 4 lines) / longer stories:
- Formality: first names, "we" or "I", slang allowed?

## Claims and rules
- Claims we can't make (results, health, money, "best", guarantees)*:
- Things legal or the client wants checked before posting:
- Paid partnerships: which brands, and always label them:
- AI-made visuals: allowed? (Photorealistic AI needs Instagram's AI label)
- Competitors or names never to mention:

## Calls to action
- What we want people to do most (book, buy, visit, DM, comment a keyword):
- Comment keywords we use (one per offer, e.g. MENU, PRICES, GUIDE):
- Links for DM offers (booking page, shop, lead magnet, price list):
- Who answers DMs after the automation (AI agent, team, owner):

## Hashtags
- Approved set (pick up to 5 per post):
- Never use:

## Approval
- Who approves posts*: agency only / client signs off / both
- Default action: save as draft / schedule directly
- Client contact for sign-off:

## Examples
- Two or three past captions the client loved (paste them):
- One caption they disliked, and why:

## Feedback log
- <date>: <what the client changed and why>
```

## Asking the questions

When a client has no file yet, ask the starred questions first, in one message, and offer to fill the rest from the client's last 10 to 20 posts. To do that, read their recent captions with `medias` `list` (`status: published`), and draft the voice, phrases, emoji habits and hashtags from what's there. Show your draft and let the agency correct it before saving.
