# The client update

Send it once the build is live (or with the go-live request, if the client approves first). It goes from the account manager, in plain language, with no platform jargon.

## Rules

- **Say what their followers and their team will see,** not how it is built. "When someone asks for a person, your team gets an email and the chat is marked Needs a human" beats "we wired the request-human scenario".
- **Name features the way the client knows them,** with the Instagram term in brackets the first time: "four quick questions under your profile (ice breakers)", "a menu in every chat (persistent menu)".
- **Fixes, one line each:** what was wrong and what happens now. Leave out platform quirks and your own earlier mistakes. Say what changed.
- **One line of "why"** only where it changes how their team works (the team hold).
- **Questions:** only what you can't decide for them, each with your suggestion.
- **Dated asks get built the day they arrive.** If the client mentions a sale or launch, build at least a minimal version that day (one line in the knowledge base, a person for everything else) and say so in the update.
- Short sentences. No hype words. No em dashes. Read it aloud once: if a line sounds like a brochure, rewrite it.
- If the account manager wants to forward it, offer it as a document they can paste into an email.

## Template

Replace everything in square brackets. Delete any section with nothing in it.

```
Subject: [Client] Instagram DMs: what's live

Hi [first name],

Here's what is now live on [@client] Instagram DMs.

What your followers will see
- [Four quick questions under your profile (ice breakers)]: "[Q1]", "[Q2]", "[Q3]" and "[Q4]". Each one gets the right answer straight away.
- [A menu in every chat (persistent menu)]: [entry 1], [entry 2], [entry 3].
- Price questions get [the price and the link / your plans with a link for each].
- Booking requests get your booking link, with one reminder if they don't open it.
- Questions get a short answer from your information. Anything it can't answer, or anyone who asks for a person, goes to your team.
- Collab and sponsorship pitches get a short "thanks, the team will look at it" and land in one list for you.
- Anyone who writes "unsubscribe" [or uses the menu entry] stops getting automated messages.

What your team will notice
- When you reply to someone by hand, the automations step back for that person until 24 hours after your last reply. To hand someone back sooner, remove them from the "Team hold (24h)" folder in Inrō.
- Put anyone you always want to handle yourselves (partners, VIPs) in the "Human only" folder. Nothing automated will message them.
- Escalations go to [email] and the "Needs a human" folder. Collab pitches go to [email] and the "Collabs: requested" folder.
- Instagram only lets you reply within 24 hours of someone's last message, so the alerts are worth answering the same day.
- [If a flow now sends something you used to type by hand, like your booking link, you can stop typing it. Replying by hand also stops the automation for that chat.]

Fixes (only on a takeover)
- [What was wrong, in one line] → [what happens now].

Questions for you
1. [Question] We suggest [suggestion].
2. [Question] We suggest [suggestion].

Thanks,
[Name]
```

## Example (a fictional local studio)

```
Subject: Instagram DMs: what's live

Hi Sam,

Here's what is now live on your Instagram DMs.

What your followers will see
- Four quick questions under your profile (ice breakers): "How much is a class?", "Can I book this week?", "Where are you?" and "Can I talk to someone?". Each one gets the right answer straight away.
- A menu in every chat (persistent menu): Book a class, Prices, Opt out of messages.
- Anyone who asks to book gets your booking link, with one reminder if they don't open it.
- Questions about classes, parking and what to bring get a short answer from your information. Anything else goes to your team.
- Brand and collab pitches get a short thank-you and land in one list.

What your team will notice
- When you reply to someone by hand, the automations step back for that person until 24 hours after your last reply. To hand someone back sooner, remove them from "Team hold (24h)".
- Regulars you always answer yourselves: add them to "Human only".
- Escalations go to the front desk email and the "Needs a human" folder.

Questions for you
1. Your price list mentions a student rate. Should the automation quote it, or send students to the front desk? We suggest quoting it, since it's on your website.
2. Over the holidays, do you want an auto-reply with your reopening date? We suggest yes, from the 23rd.

Thanks,
Alex
```
