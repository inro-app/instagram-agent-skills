# Instagram DM plan for Northside Yoga

Based on account activity from 13 October to 12 November.

## Where the account is today

- Reels do the work: the last 10 reels drew comments from 340 people. The 3 posts that asked people to comment a word drew 210 of them.
- One flow is live. It sends the class timetable to people who comment TIMETABLE on one pinned post, and 41% of the people it reached opened the link.
- 96 people replied to stories, mostly to the weekly schedule story. No flow in Inrō picks up a story reply today.
- 150 people sent a DM. About a third ask what a first class costs or whether beginners can join.
- 1,900 contacts, and 4% have an email saved here.

## Quick wins, in order

### 1. Send the intro offer to everyone who comments on reels

- **Traffic:** 340 people commented on reels in 30 days; only the pinned post has a flow.
- **Trigger:** comments containing TRIAL on any post whose caption contains TRIAL. A second trigger catches people asking for it in their own words ("how do I try a class?").
- **The DM:** "Hi! Want the intro offer? Tap below." with one button. After the tap: a link card to the intro offer page, then one reminder 3 hours later if they did not open it.
- **Leads to:** the intro offer page. People who open it are filed in "Intro offer clicked".
- **Measure:** people reached, link opens, folder size, after 2 and 4 weeks.
- **Start from:** a custom scenario, because it has two triggers. With the keyword trigger alone, the Comment to DM link wizard does it.

### 2. Answer story replies with the timetable

- **Traffic:** 96 people replied to stories in 30 days.
- **Trigger:** replies containing TIMETABLE to any story.
- **The DM:** the timetable link card straight away (a story reply already opens the conversation), one reminder if not opened.
- **Leads to:** the booking page.
- **Measure:** link opens, bookings the studio reports.
- **Start from:** template "Story replies to link clicks".

### 3. Ask for an email before the intro offer

- **Traffic:** the live flow and quick win 1 together reach a few hundred people a month, and 4% of contacts have an email.
- **Change:** add one step before the link: "Where should I send your class pass?" saving the reply as their email. People who skip it still get the link.
- **Leads to:** an email list the studio can use for the newsletter.
- **Measure:** emails collected per week.
- **Start from:** template "Collect emails from audience", or one added step on the existing flow.

## The fuller setup

**Foundations**
- Four ice breakers on the profile: "How much is a first class?", "Is it OK for beginners?", "Where are you?", "Can I book a private session?", each answered by a small flow.
- A persistent menu with "Book a class" (link) and "Ask a question".
- Opt-out on, with "stop" as the keyword.
- Folders for each stage: Commented, Intro offer clicked, Booked, Member.
- Members excluded from every lead flow.

**Automations and campaigns**
- Comment to DM on the next reel, armed before it is posted.
- A DM keyword flow for PRIVATE that asks two questions and sends the private session form.
- Like and thank kind comments on reels.
- A launch announcement campaign to the waitlist folder on the day the new studio opens.

**AI agent**
- Trained on: the website, the price list and the FAQ.
- Handles: prices, beginners, location, what to bring.
- Collects: email and preferred class time.
- Hands over to the team: injuries, refunds, anything about a membership already paid for.

## Not now

- Ad replies: no click-to-message ads are running.
- Live comment links: the studio does not go live.
- A campaign to the whole contact list: most of the list has not written in weeks, and Instagram only lets you message people who wrote in the last 24 hours.

## What we need from you

- The intro offer link and the booking page link.
- The class pass wording for the email step.
- A yes on the message copy before anything goes live.

## How we will measure

After 2 weeks and after 4 weeks: people reached by each flow, link opens, emails collected, and the bookings you tell us came from Instagram.
